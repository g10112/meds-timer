# Privacy

Meds Timer works offline.

- No account, adverts, analytics, tracking or internet permission.
- No medication names, prescriptions, contacts or location collected.
- Reminder settings, alarm sound and session information are stored on your phone only.
- Android app backup is disabled: the app does not transfer reminder sessions to a new phone.
- If you uninstall the app, its stored preferences are removed by Android.

The app requests **Notifications**, **Alarms & reminders**, full-screen notification access, vibration, wake-lock access and reboot broadcasts so it can operate its alarms. Android battery and permission settings may prevent an alert.

The app has no server, account system or data-sharing partners. Do not include health information in public GitHub issues.

This describes the published open-source app implementation, not an independent security or medical-device certification.
