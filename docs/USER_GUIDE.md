# Meds Timer — user guide

## Install

1. Open [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/g10112/meds-timer/releases/latest).
2. Download **MedsTimer.apk**, not the source-code ZIP. It works on Android 14 or newer.
3. Open the APK and follow your phone's install prompts.
4. Open **Meds Timer**, select **Complete setup** and allow notifications and exact alarms.
5. Tap **Test alarm in 10 seconds** to check sound and notifications.

Only download the APK from the official GitHub repository.

## Start your reminders

1. Check the three reminder times displayed in the app.
2. To change them, tap **Edit reminder times**. Enter durations after Start, such as 3:30, 7:00 and 10:30. They must increase and be within 48 hours.
3. Tap **Start Meds Timer**. The app shows all upcoming reminders.
4. When one rings, tap **Stop Alarm**. The others remain scheduled.

The default reminders ring 3 hours 30 minutes, 7 hours and 10 hours 30 minutes after you press Start. The times do not tell you when to take a medicine; use your own prescribed intervals.

**Cancel all remaining reminders:** tap **Cancel remaining alarms**, then confirm.

**Change the sound:** tap **Choose alarm sound** and select one on your phone.

**Add a widget:** tap **Add home-screen widget** and choose a size. Alternatively, long-press your home screen, open Widgets and select Meds Timer.

## If an alarm does not ring

- Open Meds Timer and check **Phone readiness**.
- Allow **Notifications** and **Alarms & reminders**. Also check alarm volume, battery restrictions, Do Not Disturb and full-screen alarm settings.
- Run the 10-second alarm check with your phone locked.
- If the app reports a missed reminder, check your prescribed schedule. **Do not take an extra dose solely because of the alert.**

**Changing phones:** install Meds Timer again and set your reminder times. The app does not upload or back up your reminder sessions.

**Updating:** install the newer signed APK from the same official GitHub Releases page. Do not uninstall an existing public release just to update it.

**Old preview users:** the earlier debug app and the public Meds Timer use different Android application IDs. Cancel reminders in the old copy and remove it after installing and checking the new signed app, or both apps could ring.

## Privacy and safety

No sign-in, internet access, ads or tracking. See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md).

This is a reminder utility, not a medical device or dose tracker. Phone settings and shutdowns can delay alarms. Use a second trusted reminder for time-critical medication and always follow advice from your clinician or pharmacist.

**Need help?** [Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/g10112/meds-timer/issues) with your phone model, Android version and the problem. Do not include private medical details.
