# Help improve Meds Timer

You do not need to be a developer to help.

**Found a problem?** Open an [issue](https://github.com/g10112/meds-timer/issues). Say what happened, your phone model and Android version. Do not post health information.

**Have an idea?** Open an issue and describe what you want to change.

**Want to contribute code?** Fork the repository, make one focused change and open a pull request. Explain what changed and how you checked it. Keep updates straightforward and easy for other people to use.

The project is GPL-3.0-or-later. No adverts, analytics, accounts, paywalls or data collection in the official app. Review [project principles](PROJECT_PRINCIPLES.md) before making substantial changes.
