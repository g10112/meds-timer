# Meds Timer

**Free medication reminders for Android. No ads, accounts or internet connection.**

Set three reminders, press Start, and your phone will alert you when each one is due.

**[Download Meds Timer](https://github.com/g10112/meds-timer/releases/latest)** · Android 14 or newer

## How it works

1. Install the APK from the latest official GitHub release.
2. Open the app and allow **Notifications** and **Alarms & reminders**.
3. Set your reminder intervals, then press **Start Meds Timer**.
4. When an alarm rings, press **Stop Alarm**. The remaining alarms stay scheduled.

The default reminders are **3 hours 30 minutes, 7 hours and 10 hours 30 minutes after Start**. You can change them to suit your prescribed timings. There are also four home-screen widgets and an optional alarm sound picker.

For screenshots, setup and common questions, see the **[user guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md)**.

## What you should know

- Your reminders and settings stay on your phone. The app does not collect data or connect to a server.
- It costs nothing and contains no adverts, subscriptions or paid features.
- Rebooting a phone can interrupt alerts. The app restores future reminders and warns when it detects missed reminders.
- **This is a reminder, not medical advice or a dose tracker.** Follow your prescribed schedule. Do not double up on medicine because a reminder was missed. Use a backup alarm for critical doses.
