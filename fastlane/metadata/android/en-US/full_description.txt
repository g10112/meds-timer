Meds Timer is a free, private, open-source Android reminder utility.

Press Start to schedule three reminder alarms from that moment. The defaults are 3 hours 30 minutes, 7 hours and 10 hours 30 minutes. You can change the three intervals in the app if your prescribed schedule requires different times.

Features:
- Works offline without accounts or network access.
- No advertisements, subscriptions, tracking or paid features.
- Four home-screen widget sizes.
- Choose an alarm sound and use a ten-second test alarm.
- Confirmation before cancelling remaining alarms.
- Saves timing settings locally.
- Attempts to restore remaining alarms after a phone restart.

Requires Android 14 or newer.

Important: Meds Timer is a reminder utility, not medical advice, a dosage tracker, or a medical device. Follow professional instructions, check phone alarm permissions and use a trusted backup reminder for time-critical medication. Mobile operating systems can delay or suppress alarms.
