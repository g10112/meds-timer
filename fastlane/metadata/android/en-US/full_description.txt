Meds Timer is a simple, free medication reminder app for Android.

Set three reminder intervals after you press Start. The default times are 3 hours 30 minutes, 7 hours and 10 hours 30 minutes, and you can change them to match your prescribed schedule.

Features:
- Three configurable reminder alarms
- Four home-screen widget sizes
- Sound and vibration, with a selectable alarm tone
- Confirmation before cancelling the remaining alarms
- Restores future alarms after a phone restart and warns about missed reminders
- Local settings, offline operation

No advertisements, payments, subscriptions, tracking or account creation. The app is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Android 14 or newer. Some phone settings and system restrictions may interrupt alarms, so check permissions and use a backup for time-critical medicines.

IMPORTANT: This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It is not a dose tracker and does not tell you which medication to take. Always follow your prescribed schedule and consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Never double up a dose solely because a reminder was missed.
